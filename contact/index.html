Good to know

Frequently asked

When should we book a newborn session?

Ideally during pregnancy. The session itself usually takes place 5–14 days after birth, and we pick the exact day once your baby arrives.

How long does a session take?

Children and family sessions take about 1 hour. Newborn sessions take 2–3 hours, because we pause for feeding and cuddles whenever needed.

When will we receive our photos?

Portrait sessions are delivered within 2 weeks, weddings within 6 weeks, in a private online gallery you can download from and share.

Do you travel?

Yes. Sessions within the city are included, and destination weddings are very welcome. Just ask.
